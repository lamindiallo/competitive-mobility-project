# Data licence

The data files in this folder are released under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or (at your option) any later
version. The full text is in `COPYING`.

Why GPL: league positions for 1992/93–2024/25 are derived from the engsoccerdata compilation (James Curley and contributors),
which is distributed under the GPL (version 2 or later). Derived data are therefore shared on the same terms.

Other sources and their terms:

- openfootball (England 2022/23 results; 2025/26 cross-checks): public domain (CC0).
- football-data compilations (schochastics): see that repository's terms; used for results only.
- England wage bills: figures read from statutory accounts filed at Companies House (public records).
- France wage bills: figures read from the DNCG's published annual reports on club accounts.

Each file's sources are listed on the site's Data page. If you reuse these files, please credit
"Competitive Mobility Project (Lamin Diallo)" and the original sources.
